Closet systems and practical storage

Requests can include closet systems, utility shelving, wall storage and cabinet organizers. Share what you want to store, the room dimensions and any product links. Note doors, outlets, baseboards and other details that may affect the layout.

Bookcase walls and fitted projects

Our completed work includes stacked IKEA bookcases with trim fitted along a vaulted ceiling, as well as a full-height cabinet storage wall. Product assembly, fitting, trim and custom wood organization are reviewed as distinct parts of the project so the estimate reflects the actual work.

Measurements to send

Measure the wall width, ceiling height and available depth, and include photos from more than one angle. If you already own the system, send its model and instructions. Describe any existing shelving that needs to come out and whether material purchasing or hauling is needed.

Preparing the space

Empty the work area and identify items that need to remain accessible. Let us know about stairs, parking and material delivery arrangements. The layout, materials and installation details are reviewed before the appointment is confirmed.

Serving Lone Tree and the South Denver metro

Request service in Lone Tree, Highlands Ranch, Centennial, Parker, Castle Rock or Littleton. For other locations, share your address or ZIP code so travel and availability can be reviewed.

Common questions

Can you install a closet system I purchased?

Send the model, product link, instructions and room measurements so we can review the installation.

Do you work on bookcase walls?

Yes. Our gallery includes an IKEA BILLY bookcase wall fitted with trim along a vaulted ceiling. Send photos and dimensions of your proposed project.

Do you supply materials?

Material purchasing depends on the project. We keep some materials on hand; include the system and any trim or shelf requirements in your request.
