What to include in your request

Send the television size and model, the mount model or product link, and a photo of the wall. Include the desired viewing height and whether you want a fixed, tilting or articulating setup. Tell us if the TV is currently mounted and needs to be removed first.

The wall matters as much as the TV

A drywall wall, masonry surface and fireplace surround can require different mounting methods and hardware. We review the proposed location and access before confirming the job. Share any information you have about the wall and the location of the nearest outlet.

Cable organization and outlets

Basic cable organization can be discussed with the mount placement. In-wall cable concealment, new outlets and electrical work are separate from a basic mounting request; describe those needs in advance so the appropriate scope can be determined.

Getting ready for installation

Have the television, mount, supplied hardware and instructions available. Clear the area below the proposed location and share any furniture or access constraints. Confirm the viewing position before mounting begins.

Serving Lone Tree and the South Denver metro

Request service in Lone Tree, Highlands Ranch, Centennial, Parker, Castle Rock or Littleton. For other locations, share your address or ZIP code so travel and availability can be reviewed.

Common questions

What TV sizes do you mount?

The listed TV mounting service is for televisions under 60 inches. Include the exact screen size and model in your request.

Do I need to have a mount?

Tell us whether you already have a mount and send its model or product link. Hardware and material needs are reviewed before the appointment.

Can you mount above a fireplace?

Send photos of the surround, the TV and the proposed placement. Wall construction, heat, viewing height and outlet access need to be reviewed before the job can be confirmed.
