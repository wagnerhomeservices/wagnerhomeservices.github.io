Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Wagner Home Services respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you contact us, request services, communicate with us, or use third-party platforms that connect you with Wagner Home Services.

Information We Collect

Third-Party Services

Wagner Home Services may use third-party platforms and software to manage leads, customers, payments, communications, and business records. These services may include Thumbtack, Zapier, Square, Google, and other business software providers.

These third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Wagner Home Services does not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide requested services, process payments, maintain records, or comply with applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain customer and business records for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, accounting, tax, legal, customer service, and recordkeeping purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect customer information. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact Wagner Home Services to request correction or deletion of personal information we maintain, subject to any legal, tax, accounting, payment, or recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to This Policy
