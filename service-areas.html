Our regular service area

Wagner Home Services serves Lone Tree, Highlands Ranch, Centennial, Parker, Castle Rock, Littleton and South Denver. Share your city and ZIP code when requesting an estimate so we can review travel and appointment availability.

Colorado Springs and mountain locations

Most services are considered within approximately a 1.5-hour drive of Lone Tree. Requests from Colorado Springs, Pine and communities along the I-70 corridor are reviewed individually. Access, weather, travel distance and the project scope can affect scheduling.

What to send with your location

Include your ZIP code, photos, product links or measurements, and any parking, stairs or access constraints. For hauling, include photos of the full load. For assembly or installation, let us know whether materials have arrived and whether they are already in the work area.
